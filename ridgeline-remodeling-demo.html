How It Works

From first call to final walkthrough.

Every project follows the same five steps — no surprises.

01
Free Consultation
+

We walk the space with you, talk through your goals and budget, and take initial measurements. No obligation, no pressure.

02
Design & Estimate
+

We put together a design direction and a detailed, itemized estimate — you'll know exactly what you're paying for before signing anything.

03
Permits & Materials
+

We pull all required permits and order materials, so there's zero delay once the crew shows up on day one.

04
Build
+

Our crew works your project start to finish — we send daily photo updates so you always know where things stand.

05
Final Walkthrough
+

We walk the finished space with you, handle any punch-list items on the spot, and hand over care instructions and warranty info.
