About

Coached, not crowded.

Groundwork Strength Club started in 2019 with one idea: most gyms are built for machines, not for people. We built ours around small groups and real coaching instead.

Every session caps at eight people, so our coaches can actually watch your form, adjust your program, and know your name. No wandering between machines wondering what's next — just a plan, a coach, and the work.

Whether you're chasing your first pull-up or training for a meet, the program bends to you. We track load, reps, and progress every session, so you can see the work adding up.
