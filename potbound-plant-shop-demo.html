About

We've killed plants too.

Potbound started because our founder killed four fiddle leaf figs in a row before finding out her apartment barely got any light at all. Turns out most "plant person" advice assumes you have a sunroom. Most people don't.

So we built a shop — and the matcher above — around matching real plants to real living conditions, not Instagram conditions. No shame if your last plant didn't make it. We'll find you one that will.

Care 101

Questions we get every day.

Almost always overwatering, not underwatering. Let the soil dry out fully before the next watering, and check the pot has drainage — soggy roots turn leaves yellow faster than dry ones.

Yes, with the right pick. ZZ plants, snake plants, and pothos all tolerate low or artificial light. Skip anything labeled "bright indirect light required" if your space is genuinely dim.

Every tag in the shop lists toxicity clearly. As a rule, monstera, pothos, and philodendron are not pet-safe if chewed — calathea, pilea, and most palms are.
